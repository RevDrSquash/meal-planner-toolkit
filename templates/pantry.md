# Pantry

Items listed here are assumed to be on hand. The agent excludes them from
the shopping list and instead lists them in a **Pantry check** section on
each meal plan so you can confirm before ordering.

**The list below is a default, not an inventory of your kitchen.** It is
what most households keep. Onboarding shows it to you once and asks what
to strike or add — that is faster than starting empty and discovering the
gaps one shopping list at a time.

Getting this wrong is cheap in one direction and annoying in the other: an
item listed here that you do not actually have goes missing at the stove,
so strike anything you are unsure about.

## How to use

- Edit this file when usual stock changes (add, remove, or rename).
- Tell the agent when you are temporarily out of something — it will add
  that item for this order without changing this file.
- Preferred store products belong in `shopping/product-mappings.md`.
