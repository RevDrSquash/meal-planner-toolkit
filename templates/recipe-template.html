Weeknight Chili

Serves 4 · 45 min · beef, one-pot, freezer-friendly

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 can (796 ml) diced tomatoes
  • 1 can (540 ml) red kidney beans
  • 2 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin

Nutrition

Per serving

Calories480 kcal
Protein32 g
Fat22 g
Carbohydrates34 g

Instructions

  1. Step 1

    Brown the ground beef in a large pot over medium-high heat, then drain.

  2. Step 2

    Add onion and garlic; cook until softened, about 5 minutes.

  3. Step 3

    Stir in chili powder and cumin and cook for 1 minute.

  4. Step 4

    Add diced tomatoes and kidney beans. Simmer for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.

  5. Step 5

    Season to taste and serve.

Notes

Pantry staples (spices, oil) usually don't need to go on the shopping list.
