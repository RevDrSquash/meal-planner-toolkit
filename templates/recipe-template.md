# Weeknight Chili

- Serves: 4
- Time: 45 min
- Tags: beef, one-pot, freezer-friendly

## Ingredients

- 500 g ground beef
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 can (796 ml) diced tomatoes
- 1 can (540 ml) red kidney beans
- 2 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin

## Nutrition

- Calories: 480 kcal
- Protein: 32 g
- Fat: 22 g
- Carbohydrates: 34 g

## Instructions

1. Brown the ground beef in a large pot over medium-high heat, then drain.
2. Add onion and garlic; cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
3. Stir in chili powder and cumin and cook for 1 minute.
4. Add diced tomatoes and kidney beans. Simmer for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
5. Season to taste and serve.

## Notes

Pantry staples (spices, oil) usually don't need to go on the shopping list.
