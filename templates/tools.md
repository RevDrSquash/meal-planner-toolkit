# Kitchen tools

Durable notes about cooking equipment and capacity that affect recipe
selection and cooking-session planning. This file starts mostly empty and
accumulates details over time.

Do not inventory a normal home kitchen. Record notable exceptions and
specialty equipment only, for example:

- no oven, unusually small oven, or no stovetop
- unusually large mixing bowls
- specialty appliances (pressure cooker, stand mixer, outdoor grill)

Preferred store and dietary notes belong in `preferences.md`.
